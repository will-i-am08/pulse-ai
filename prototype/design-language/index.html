Guardrails

Do / don’t

Do

  • Put disc + Kip on every poster — name is brand architecture
  • Derive marks from whiskers, arcs, ticks, ears
  • Keep the disc cream and the cat charcoal — always
  • Put type on a solid band (≥40% on Kin posters)
  • Sell life after social is handled

Don’t

  • Draw a second cat into the plate
  • Drop the wordmark and rely on the disc alone on posters
  • Invert, recolour, or bubble the logo
  • Let abstract art become a different brand
  • Use Polsia orange / Lindy blue as chrome
  • Lead with product mechanics over relief
