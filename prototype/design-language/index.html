Guardrails

Do / don’t

Do

  • Sell life after Kip — relief, handled, off their mind
  • Lead with abstract art that feels like mental quiet
  • Keep the disc unaltered as the studio seal
  • Use 2–4 mate-voice words that name the outcome
  • Keep “cheap + completely done” in the whisper, not a feature grid

Don’t

  • Lead with how it works (SMS, AI, approve flow)
  • Sound like a platform, suite, or “AI-powered” deck
  • Bring back neon volt bars or glow
  • Redraw cats into the abstract field
  • Trap art in rounded SaaS cards
