Guardrails

Do / don’t

Do

  • Lead with Kip + one outcome claim + one CTA
  • Use Series 04 plates for new posters and posts
  • Keep ≥40% solid type band; type can be the monument
  • Billboard the feed like a poster wall
  • Keep the disc unaltered as the studio seal

Don’t

  • Lead with SMS / AI / approve-flow mechanics
  • Wear Polsia orange, Times, terminal, or cult copy
  • Wear Lindy blue PLG chrome or UI-first heroes
  • Let brushwork cut through letterforms
  • Trap art in rounded SaaS cards
