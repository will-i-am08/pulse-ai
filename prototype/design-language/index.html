Guardrails

Do / don’t

Do

  • Lead with the disc — brand survives without the nav
  • One claim per frame, readable across a room
  • Keep volt as a signal (underline, tick, ready chip)
  • Use real shop photography, full-bleed
  • Quiet paper UI in-product; loud void on marketing

Don’t

  • Recolour or invert the cat; no chat bubble on the mark
  • Copy Polsia’s Times / terminal / orange kit
  • Wash posters in purple gradients or cream-serif terracotta
  • Fill the hero with stats, cards, or pill clusters
  • Write threat copy (“never hire again”)
