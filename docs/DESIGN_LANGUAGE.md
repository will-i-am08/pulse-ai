## 0. v5 — stay on-brand with the logo

Feedback after v4: abstract plates stopped scroll, but they could have belonged to any charcoal brand. **v5 pulls marketing back to the mark.**

| Logo fact | Marketing rule |
|---|---|
| Charcoal cat on disc `#f4f1ea` | Paper / plate ground is **exact disc cream** |
| Dry-brush strokes `#202020` | Plates use the **same stroke weight and tooth** |
| Whiskers, closed-eye arcs, forehead ticks, ear arches | Series 05 **Kin** remixes those marks — never a second full cat |
| Disc is the brand | Seal is **readable**; lockups stay product-faithful |

**Still true from v4:** Polsia stop-scroll physics + Lindy outcome clarity. The *costume* is Kip’s hand.

## 0b. What v4 learned (Polsia × Lindy)

| Source | Steal (physics) | Don’t steal (costume) |
|---|---|---|
| **[Polsia](https://polsia.com/)** | One claim owns the frame · hard contrast · wheatpaste density · type as architecture · proof whisper · outcome over UI | Times · terminal · safety orange · cult doctrine · “never hire” · glitch cinema |
| **[Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai/)** | One clear hero promise · relief framing · social as done-for-you proof · demos after the feeling | Royal-blue PLG chrome · integration-orbit heroes · UI-first heroes |

> **Polsia’s stop-scroll physics + Lindy’s outcome clarity + Kip’s paper/charcoal hand.**

Sell: **cheap social, completely handled — off their mind.**

## 3. Kip anchors (unchanged)

From [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md):

- Mark: hand-drawn charcoal cat on circular disc `#f4f1ea`
- Do not invert, recolour, bubble, or tech-ify the cat
- Wordmark: **Kip**, weight 600, tight tracking
- Product chrome: near-black / paper
- Voice: switched-on mate — warm, sharp, owns being AI

---

## 4. Concept: Charcoal Field

**One sentence:** Quiet art from the **same hand as the cat**; the disc is the brand; the line is the relief.

1. **Mark** — disc, unaltered, **readable** (hero-scale when it’s the brand signal).
2. **Field** — Kin plates first; relief plates for heavier night moods.
3. **Line** — short outcome type; on posters, type is architecture.

### Composition law

1. Art zone + solid type band (**≥40%** on Series 04/05).
2. Object-position top.
3. No stroke through glyphs.
4. Billboard template — only plate + line change.
5. Type as architecture on manifesto posters.
6. Seal lives with type — readable size.
7. **Kin before generic charcoal.**
8. Scrim last resort.

### Plate rules

1. Full-bleed.  
2. No figurative cats in the plate.  
3. Match logo dry-brush tooth.  
4. Paper = `#f4f1ea`. Charcoal = `#202020`.  
5. Never neon.

Brief: *“Abstract dry-brush charcoal on cream #f4f1ea, same stroke weight as a hand-drawn cat logo — whiskers, closed-eye arcs, forehead ticks, ear arches — NOT a cat face, empty lower type zone ≥40%, no text, no neon, [ratio].”*

## 7. Typography

| Role | Direction |
|---|---|
| Display | `Syne` 700–800 |
| Poster line | 2–5 words, wheatpaste scale |
| Supporting | `DM Sans` / SF Pro |
| Wordmark | SF Pro / system 600 |

Outcome lines: `SOCIAL’S HANDLED.` · `OFF YOUR MIND.` · `POSTED. YOU’RE FREE.` — not feature mechanics.

---

## 8. Poster & post grammar

1. Full-bleed Kin (or relief) plate  
2. Disc in type band (readable)  
3. One line (+ whisper)  
4. No stats strip / feature pills  

**Landing first viewport:** large Kip disc · one headline · one sentence · one CTA · one Kin plate. Full-bleed. No card grid.

**Social:** still plates + type; wall density; disc on every tile.

**Don’t:** invert the cat · second cat in art · Polsia orange/Times · Lindy blue/UI hero · rounded SaaS card traps.

---

## 9. Motion

Settle plate → arrive type → seal last. No bounce. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## 10. Product vs marketing

| Surface | Language |
|---|---|
| App / auth / legal | `DESIGN.md` quiet system |
| Landing / OG / ads / posters / social | Charcoal Field Kin + Syne |
| SMS | Voice rules — don’t fight them |

---

## 11. Rollout

1. **Series 05 Kin is default** for new posters/posts.  
2. Landing: large disc on `#f4f1ea` + `plate-kin-wide` / hand + outcome line.  
3. Social: smile + whisk + hand — disc readable every tile.  
4. Promote into `DESIGN.md` when approved.

---

## 12. Checklist

1. Life after Kip — or how Kip works?  
2. Relief in under a second?  
3. Looks like the **logo’s hand** — or a different studio?  
4. Disc readable and unaltered?  
5. Marks from whiskers / arcs / ticks / ears?  
6. Type band ≥40%?  
7. No Polsia orange/cult, no Lindy blue/UI-hero?
