# Brand Voice Guide — "Ember & Oak" (café)

_Platforms analysed: instagram, facebook, threads · 148 posts · 24 images_

## Snapshot
Warm, unhurried, a little cheeky. Writes like the owner leaning over the counter
for a chat — short lines, lowercase starts, one emoji if any. Never salesy, never
corporate. If a caption feels like an ad, it's wrong.

## Tone & Personality
- Friendly and grounded — talks to regulars, not "customers"
- Quietly proud of the craft (beans, bakes) without boasting
- Gently playful; leans on understatement over hype
- Community-first: names locals, seasons, the weather, the street

## Writing Mechanics
- **Emoji:** ~4 in 10 posts (0.5 per post). Almost always a single trailing emoji.
  Favourites: ☕ 🌿 🥐. Never mid-sentence, never more than one.
- **Exclamation marks:** rare — roughly 1 in 6 posts, and never more than one.
- **Ellipses:** a signature move (~1 in 4 posts) for a soft, trailing thought…
- **Capitalisation:** lowercase starts on most casual posts; sentence case for
  announcements. Never all-caps shouting.
- **Sentence length:** short and punchy — median 9 words. Often fragments.
- **Openers:** "new on the counter…", "rainy one today", "quick one —"
- **Sign-offs:** "see you soon x", "come say hi", occasionally just the emoji
- **Hashtags:** sparing (avg 1.8/post), always trailing, local + craft tags:
  #specialtycoffee #[suburb]coffee #brunch
- **CTAs:** soft and low-pressure — "pop in", "grab one before they go",
  never "BUY NOW" or "link in bio!!!"
- **Phrases they actually use:** "the good stuff", "small batch", "on the counter",
  "worth the walk"

## Photo & Visual Style
- **Aesthetic:** warm, natural, lived-in — never staged-stocky
- **Lighting:** soft natural window light; morning golden tones
- **Composition:** overhead flat-lays of the counter + close, shallow-depth shots
  of a single cup or bake
- **Colour palette:** warm browns, cream, sage green, timber
- **Editing:** light, warm, slightly lifted shadows; minimal filter
- **Common subjects:** coffee cups, pastries, hands, the timber counter, greenery
- **Recurring motifs:** latte art from above, steam, a sprig of something green

## Do
- Keep it short, warm and human
- Lead with the thing (the bake, the bean, the moment), not the sell
- Use lowercase for casual posts; one emoji max
- Name the local, the season, the weather

## Don't
- No hype words ("amazing!!!", "must-try", "obsessed")
- No stacked hashtags or emoji spam
- No corporate voice or third-person ("Ember & Oak is proud to…")
- Never more than one exclamation mark

## Example Captions (their real voice)
- "new on the counter — a brown butter pear number that did not last long yesterday ☕"
- "rainy one today. the kind of morning the corner seat was made for…"
- "small batch guatemalan just landed. bright, jammy, worth the walk 🌿"
- "quick one — kitchen's doing sausage rolls again saturday. come say hi x"
