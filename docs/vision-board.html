The Agent, On One Page

A small business owner sends a photo — or nothing at all — and never thinks about social media again. Pulse runs the whole presence: posting, replies, reviews and leads, on brand, in one text thread.

Architecture & flow · current as of 08/09/2026 · branch main + PR #13

The Loop — how it works

01 · CAPTURE

Listen everywhere

  • Owner texts a photo/video via Discord, SMS, Linq
  • Meta webhooks catch IG/FB comments, DMs, mentions
  • GBP sync pulls Google reviews hourly
  • Media bytes stored instantly — never a temp URL
02 · THINK

Draft & triage

  • Claude drafts on-brand captions in the client's voice
  • Inbound sorted: auto safe replies · draft judgment calls · escalate complaints & hot leads
  • Spam hidden, never shown to the owner
  • Every edit teaches the voice profile
03 · APPROVE

Owner taps once

  • Drafts land in the single chat thread
  • Dashboard inbox for replies, calendar for posts
  • Approve, edit, or dismiss — nothing publishes without a tap
04 · PUBLISH

Post & reply

  • Worker publishes on schedule — smart windows, caps, spacing
  • Instagram live · Facebook & Google as approvals land
  • Replies posted back; failures surface loudly, never silent
05 · LEARN & NUDGE

Compounds weekly

  • Corrections fold into brand voice
  • Check-ins, performance reports, gap-fill content
  • Sentiment-spike alarm on negativity surges
  • Competitor watch + content plans
⛔ Approval is absolute. No code path publishes or replies without a logged approval — every draft, edit, send and failure is audited.
